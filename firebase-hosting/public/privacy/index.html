Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 1, 2026  •  Last Updated: March 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how AEONIMUS ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our AI music publishing platform and our use of Google APIs, including the YouTube Data API v3.

⚠ Google API Services — Required Disclosure

AEONIMUS's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data obtained via Google OAuth is used solely to perform the YouTube publishing actions explicitly authorized by the user and for no other purpose.

1. Who We Are

AEONIMUS is an AI music publishing platform operated for the purpose of cataloging and distributing AI-generated audio content to YouTube channels owned and operated by authorized AEONIMUS account holders. This platform is not a general consumer service. Access is restricted to the channel owner(s) who have explicitly authorized AEONIMUS to act on their behalf.

For questions about this policy, contact us at: contact@aeonimus.ai

2. Google API Scopes We Request

When you authorize AEONIMUS using your Google account, we request the following OAuth 2.0 scopes:

  • ✓

    https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload

    Allows AEONIMUS to upload video content to the authorized YouTube channel on your behalf.

  • ✓

    https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube

    Allows AEONIMUS to manage video metadata (titles, descriptions, tags, privacy status) on videos uploaded by this application.

We request only the minimum permissions necessary to perform the publishing functions you authorize. We do not request access to your YouTube watch history, subscriptions, private playlists, or any channels other than the one you explicitly authorize.

3. Information We Collect

From Google OAuth authorization, we receive and store:

  • OAuth access token and refresh token for the authorized channel
  • YouTube channel ID and channel name associated with the authorized account

From our own platform operations, we collect:

  • AI-generated audio files and associated metadata (titles, creation dates, tags)
  • YouTube video IDs and upload status for videos published through this platform
  • Platform activity logs (upload timestamps, error logs) for operational purposes

We do not collect passwords, payment information, personal addresses, or any Google account data beyond what is listed above.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Uploading AI-generated video content to your authorized YouTube channel
  • Setting video titles, descriptions, tags, and AI-content disclosure labels on uploaded videos
  • Tracking upload status and maintaining an internal catalog of published content
  • Diagnosing and resolving technical errors in the publishing pipeline

We do not use your Google account data for advertising, analytics profiling, training AI models, or any purpose beyond the direct YouTube publishing functions described above.

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing, advertising, or commercial purposes of any kind.

Data may be stored using Google Cloud Platform services (Google Cloud Storage, Cloud Run) operated under Google's infrastructure. All data remains within Google Cloud environments subject to Google Cloud's Data Processing Addendum.

We may disclose data if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users, as permitted by applicable law.

6. Data Retention

OAuth tokens are retained only as long as necessary to perform active publishing operations. You may revoke AEONIMUS's access to your YouTube account at any time by visiting Google Account Permissions and removing AEONIMUS from authorized applications.

Upon revocation, we will cease all API calls using your credentials. Residual operational logs are retained for up to 90 days for debugging purposes and then permanently deleted.

7. Your Rights and Controls

  • Revoke Access: Remove AEONIMUS's authorization at any time via Google Account Permissions.
  • Data Deletion: Request deletion of any data we hold by emailing contact@aeonimus.ai. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.
  • Data Access: Request a copy of the data we hold associated with your channel by contacting us at the email above.

8. Security

OAuth tokens are stored in Google Cloud Secret Manager using industry-standard encryption at rest and in transit. Access to credentials is restricted to the AEONIMUS publishing pipeline processes and is not accessible to unauthorized parties.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of AEONIMUS after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

For questions, concerns, or data requests regarding this Privacy Policy:

Email: contact@aeonimus.ai
